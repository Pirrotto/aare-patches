# AARE Patches

[Morphe](https://morphe.software) patches for [AARE](https://github.com/prototux/AARE), the open-source Android Auto replacement. They add a few things for driving: WhatsApp voice messages and voice replies in the car, and navigation that keeps going in tunnels. Each one can be turned on or off in AARE's settings.

**For AARE 1.6.0.** The patches only accept the official AARE release, signed with the key published in AARE's README.

## 🩹 The patches

| Patch | What it does | Needs |
|---|---|---|

| **Voice messages** | When AARE reads a message aloud, WhatsApp voice messages play in the car instead of "Voice message (0:05)". Off by default. | Shizuku, allowed for AARE (as for car audio) |

Dictate (with punctuation on Android 13+), hear it read back, say "yes" or tap **Send**. Off by default. | Voice messages patch; with AARE's *Car microphone for phone apps* on it listens through the car |
| **Car radio pause** | While AARE reads a message aloud and during a voice reply, the car's radio pauses instead of only getting lower, until AARE is done. Optionally also while a phone app records. Off by default. | Voice messages patch |
| **Assistant on the car screen** | When an assistant's car app doesn't answer AARE (Google's only answers Android Auto), the voice button opens it as an assistant without a car app: on the car screen with AARE's own option, else on the phone. Optionally also while driving. Off by default. | For the car screen: AARE's *Open assistants without a car app on the car screen* and Shizuku |

### Tunnel navigation

With *Use the car's GPS*, AARE gives the phone the car's position. In a tunnel the car has no fix, so the navigation app (Vela, Maps, Waze…) stops. The car still sends its speed, compass and gyroscope: from the last good fix the patch estimates where the car is, once a second. The gyroscope's direction is learned from the GPS while driving, so it works in any car, and a compass that freezes in the tunnel is ignored. Other apps get this position too, so it reports an honest accuracy (the last fix's plus 10 % of the distance driven without GPS, as measured in tunnels) and stops once that would pass 45 m, about 400 m into a tunnel at first (navigation apps such as Vela ignore positions worse than 50 m, and one far off the road made Vela reroute again and again); it also stops after 5 km or 5 minutes of driving, and standing in a queue doesn't reset it. In longer tunnels the arrow then waits for the exit, as without the patch. What it learns about the car (the gyroscope's direction, how much to turn in curves, the speed scale; also the gyroscope's zero offset on straights, only for the diagnostics) is kept in AARE's private storage for each car model, as reported by the car (at most 5), so the next trip starts with it; no position is kept. Switching the patch off deletes it. Below it, the test switch *Location check* (off by default, needs Shizuku) notes in the diagnostics, for 5 minutes after the car connects, whether AARE and the navigation apps (Vela, Organic Maps, CoMaps, OsmAnd) may read the location: only their process state, no position. It is for the black map at the start of a drive. The cause, found with it on 24.11: when the car connects over Bluetooth, AARE's service starts in the background, and Android then doesn't let it read the location "while in use" (AARE itself still can, with *Allow all the time*), so it can't pass that right to the navigation app it shows (Vela has "while in use" only): no location until AARE or Vela is opened on the phone. The second test switch, *Location for navigation at the start* (off by default, needs Shizuku), has the Shizuku helper, as the shell, start a small location service inside AARE (`LocationBridgeService`) while the car is connected: Android lets a foreground service started by the shell read the location while in use, as with Android Auto's own start, and the right then belongs to AARE's whole process. (Sending AARE's own start request through the shell, tried first, did not help: a request runs as the app that made it.) The bridge is open only to the system and the shell (permission `INTERACT_ACROSS_USERS_FULL`, as AARE's own Shizuku provider); it reads and returns nothing, stops at once if no car is connected and when the car disconnects, and shows an AARE notification while it runs. The helper starts it only for AARE itself; no other app's permissions are changed.

### Voice messages

WhatsApp keeps voice notes in a folder other apps can't read. The patch reads them through Shizuku with a small helper that answers one question only: *"the voice message received at this time, lasting this long?"*. The helper checks WhatsApp's voice notes itself and hands AARE at most that one recording, read-only: AARE never sees a list, a file name or any other voice note. WhatsApp keeps sent and received voice notes together, so a recording counts only if it was saved right after the message arrived and lasts what the notification says: a voice note you sent, or another chat's, is never played. When unsure it says "voice message". No storage permission.

### Voice reply

Works with WhatsApp and any messaging app whose notifications can be answered. Nothing is sent without your confirmation: what you dictated is read back first, and a "no" always wins. To dictate it again, say "change message" or "retry" ("cambia messaggio", "riprova", "rifai", "sbagliato", "nochmal"). It uses the phone's speech recognition; with AARE's *Car microphone for phone apps* on, that hears the car's microphone.

**Car microphone for dictation (test)** (setting of the patch, off by default, Android 13+): the speech recognition is given AARE's own microphone for car apps, the car's when it has one, instead of opening a microphone itself. It is for cars where "Car microphone for phone apps" doesn't reach the recognizer, and the reply answered "I didn't catch that". With an audio of its own the recognizer stops only when that audio ends, so the patch ends it once the words have stayed the same for 2.5 s (1.5 s for a yes/no). The audio goes only to the recognizer; the diagnostics note only how many KB went to it (none means the recognizer used its own microphone).

**Ask by voice to reply** (setting of the patch, off by default): after the reading AARE asks "Do you want to reply?" ("Vuoi rispondere?") and listens a few seconds, with the music paused. "Yes" / "sì" / "rispondi" starts the dictation, "no" closes the card; silence leaves the Reply card as before.

### Car radio pause

AARE asks the car to only lower its other sound for anything it plays on the navigation channel, and gives it back 3 s after each phrase: on some cars (a Skoda Octavia) the radio then stays fairly loud and comes back up between the questions of a voice reply. With this patch on, while AARE itself speaks (a message read aloud, "Do you want to reply?", the listening, the read-back) it asks the car for a short pause instead, as Android Auto does for its assistant, and keeps it until AARE is done (at most 3 minutes). Navigation prompts from Vela, Maps and the others are sent as before. On some cars the radio may pause rather than get lower: that is what Android Auto does too.

**Also while an app records** (setting of the patch, off by default): while a car is connected and a phone app records (a WhatsApp voice message, a recorder, dictation, an assistant listening), the car's own radio (FM/DAB) pauses too, as with Android Auto, and comes back 1.5 s after the recording ends (at the latest after 10 minutes, or when the car disconnects). AARE's own *Pause while an app records* pauses only the phone's music; this pauses the car's sound. Calls are left to the car, and apps that Android silences in the background don't count. Android tells AARE that something records and from which source, never which app: no permission, nothing kept.

### Assistant on the car screen

AARE opens an assistant with a car app inside its car screen. Google's assistant has one, but it only answers Android Auto, so the voice button waited and AARE gave up after 10 s. With this patch on, if the car app isn't up after 5 s (or AARE gives up on it), the waiting bar closes and AARE opens the assistant as it opens one without a car app: with AARE's *Open assistants without a car app on the car screen* (Assistant settings, needs Shizuku) its own window appears in a panel on the car screen, otherwise on the phone. For the rest of the trip the voice button goes straight there. Its voice comes from the car's speakers (AARE plays the assistant's sound in the car); with AARE's *Car microphone for phone apps* it can hear the car's microphone. AARE shows that panel only while parked; the sub-option **Also while driving** (off by default) keeps it while driving too, touchable, like Android Auto's assistant bar. Only the car app's package name is kept, in memory, never in logs.

### Map area check

AARE tells the navigation app which part of the map its cards leave free, and the app centres its arrow there. Sometimes the route moves towards a card instead. With this test switch on, each new free area is noted in the diagnostics: the map, the cards over it and the free part, as pixel rectangles (left,top-right,bottom), at most 20 lines a minute. Only sizes; no position and nothing of what the cards show.

## 📲 How to use

1. In Morphe, add this repository as a patch source: the button below, or `https://github.com/Pirrotto/aare-patches`.

   <a href="https://morphe.software/add-source?github=Pirrotto/aare-patches">
     <img src=".github/assets/add-to-morphe.svg" alt="Add to Morphe" height="48">
   </a>

2. Download the official `aare-1.6.0.apk` from the [AARE releases](https://github.com/prototux/AARE/releases) and patch it in Morphe.
3. Uninstall the official AARE (the patched app has a different signature), install the patched one and set AARE up.
4. Turn the patches on in **AARE → Settings → Settings** (the last row).
5. For Voice messages: keep [Shizuku](https://github.com/RikkaApps/Shizuku) running and allowed for AARE.

Patching again later (a new release of the patches) installs over the patched AARE and keeps its settings.

## ✅ In AARE itself

Some patches were proposed to AARE and are now part of it, so they are no longer here:

- **Background location** (navigation with the phone locked): AARE 1.6.0, [#351](https://github.com/prototux/AARE/pull/351).
- **Car microphone** for dictation: AARE 1.6.0's *Car microphone for phone apps*, [#256](https://github.com/prototux/AARE/pull/256).
- **Reply by voice**: proposed for AARE 1.6.1, [#353](https://github.com/prototux/AARE/pull/353).

Tunnel navigation and Voice messages are tested here first, then proposed to AARE.

The patches for **AARE 1.5.0** (with the AARE assistant and Integrated Shizuku) are in [v1.10.6](https://github.com/Pirrotto/aare-patches/releases/tag/v1.10.6), the last release for 1.5.0.

New in the patches' Settings, under Tunnel navigation: "Tunnel maps" downloads, on a tap, the road tunnels of the countries you choose (OpenStreetMap, a few KB per country, signed and checked before use, from this project's GitHub page only; the request names only the country's file, never your position; or "From file…" loads a country's file (such as `Switzerland.aaretun`, the map with its signature) downloaded from the [tunnel maps page](https://github.com/Pirrotto/aare-patches/releases/tag/tunnel-maps), checked the same way). Voice reply has a new test setting, "Car microphone for dictation" (off by default, Android 13+): the speech recognition hears the car's microphone directly, for when it answered "I didn't catch that".

## 🧑‍💻 Development

- Day-to-day work goes to `dev`: every push runs the unit tests, builds the patches and applies them to the official AARE APK, and keeps the patches file as a private test build (an artifact of the run, not a release).
- Public releases come from `main`, at most twice a week (Thursday evening, Saturday evening or Sunday morning), after testing in the car. Commits follow [semantic commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org) (`feat:`, `fix:`, `chore:`).
- Local build: `./gradlew buildAndroid`, then `patches/build/libs/patches-*.mpp`.

Based on the [Morphe patches template](https://github.com/MorpheApp/morphe-patches-template). Not affiliated with Morphe or with AARE.
