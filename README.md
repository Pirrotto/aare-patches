# AARE Patches

[Morphe](https://morphe.software) patches for [AARE](https://github.com/prototux/AARE), the open-source Android Auto replacement. They add a few things for driving: WhatsApp voice messages and voice replies in the car, and navigation that keeps going in tunnels. Each one can be turned on or off in AARE's settings.

**For AARE 1.6.0.** The patches only accept the official AARE release, signed with the key published in AARE's README.

## 🩹 The patches

| Patch | What it does | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Tunnel navigation** | In tunnels the position keeps moving, estimated from the car's speed, compass and gyroscope, until the GPS is back. Off by default. | AARE's *Use the car's GPS* on, a car that sends its GPS |
| **Voice messages** | When AARE reads a message aloud, WhatsApp voice messages play in the car instead of "Voice message (0:05)". Off by default. | Shizuku, allowed for AARE (as for car audio) |
| **Voice reply** | After a message is read aloud: a **Reply** card for 15 s and **Reply** in the notification centre. Dictate (with punctuation on Android 13+), hear it read back, say "yes" or tap **Send**. Off by default. | Voice messages patch; with AARE's *Car microphone for phone apps* on it listens through the car |
| **Car radio pause** | While AARE reads a message aloud and during a voice reply, the car's radio pauses instead of only getting lower, until AARE is done. Off by default. | Voice messages patch |
| **Settings** | A **Settings** row at the end of AARE's settings, with one switch per patch. | Included with every patch |

### Tunnel navigation

With *Use the car's GPS*, AARE gives the phone the car's position. In a tunnel the car has no fix, so the navigation app (Vela, Maps, Waze…) stops. The car still sends its speed, compass and gyroscope: from the last good fix the patch estimates where the car is, once a second. The gyroscope's direction is learned from the GPS while driving, so it works in any car, and a compass that freezes in the tunnel is ignored. Other apps get this position too, so it reports an honest accuracy (the last fix's plus 10 % of the distance driven without GPS, as measured in tunnels) and stops once that would pass 45 m, about 400 m into a tunnel (navigation apps such as Vela ignore positions worse than 50 m, and one far off the road made Vela reroute again and again); it also stops after 5 km or 5 minutes of driving, and standing in a queue doesn't reset it. In longer tunnels the arrow then waits for the exit, as without the patch. What it learns about the car (the gyroscope's direction, how much to turn in curves, the speed scale; also the gyroscope's zero offset on straights, only for the diagnostics) is kept in AARE's private storage for each car model, as reported by the car (at most 5), so the next trip starts with it; no position is kept. Switching the patch off deletes it. Below it, the test switch *Location check* (off by default, needs Shizuku) notes in the diagnostics, for 5 minutes after the car connects, whether AARE and the navigation apps (Vela, Organic Maps, CoMaps, OsmAnd) may read the location: only their process state, no position. It is for the black map at the start of a drive. The cause, found with it on 24.11: when the car connects over Bluetooth, AARE's service starts in the background, and Android then doesn't let it read the location "while in use" (AARE itself still can, with *Allow all the time*), so it can't pass that right to the navigation app it shows (Vela has "while in use" only): no location until AARE or Vela is opened on the phone. The second test switch, *Location for navigation at the start* (off by default, needs Shizuku), has the Shizuku helper, as the shell, start a small location service inside AARE (`LocationBridgeService`) while the car is connected: Android lets a foreground service started by the shell read the location while in use, as with Android Auto's own start, and the right then belongs to AARE's whole process. (Sending AARE's own start request through the shell, tried first, did not help: a request runs as the app that made it.) The bridge is open only to the system and the shell (permission `INTERACT_ACROSS_USERS_FULL`, as AARE's own Shizuku provider); it reads and returns nothing, stops at once if no car is connected and when the car disconnects, and shows an AARE notification while it runs. The helper starts it only for AARE itself; no other app's permissions are changed.

### Voice messages

WhatsApp keeps voice notes in a folder other apps can't read. The patch reads them through Shizuku with a small helper that answers one question only: *"the voice message received at this time, lasting this long?"*. The helper checks WhatsApp's voice notes itself and hands AARE at most that one recording, read-only: AARE never sees a list, a file name or any other voice note. WhatsApp keeps sent and received voice notes together, so a recording counts only if it was saved right after the message arrived and lasts what the notification says: a voice note you sent, or another chat's, is never played. When unsure it says "voice message". No storage permission.

### Voice reply

Works with WhatsApp and any messaging app whose notifications can be answered. Nothing is sent without your confirmation: what you dictated is read back first, and a "no" always wins. To dictate it again, say "change message" or "retry" ("cambia messaggio", "riprova", "rifai", "sbagliato", "nochmal"). It uses the phone's speech recognition; with AARE's *Car microphone for phone apps* on, that hears the car's microphone.

**Ask by voice to reply** (setting of the patch, off by default): after the reading AARE asks "Do you want to reply?" ("Vuoi rispondere?") and listens a few seconds, with the music paused. "Yes" / "sì" / "rispondi" starts the dictation, "no" closes the card; silence leaves the Reply card as before.

### Car radio pause

AARE asks the car to only lower its other sound for anything it plays on the navigation channel, and gives it back 3 s after each phrase: on some cars (a Skoda Octavia) the radio then stays fairly loud and comes back up between the questions of a voice reply. With this patch on, while AARE itself speaks (a message read aloud, "Do you want to reply?", the listening, the read-back) it asks the car for a short pause instead, as Android Auto does for its assistant, and keeps it until AARE is done (at most 3 minutes). Navigation prompts from Vela, Maps and the others are sent as before. On some cars the radio may pause rather than get lower: that is what Android Auto does too.

## 📲 How to use

1. In Morphe, add this repository as a patch source: the button below, or `https://github.com/Pirrotto/aare-patches`.

   <a href="https://morphe.software/add-source?github=Pirrotto/aare-patches">
     <img src=".github/assets/add-to-morphe.svg" alt="Add to Morphe" height="48">
   </a>

2. Download the official `aare-1.6.0.apk` from the [AARE releases](https://github.com/prototux/AARE/releases) and patch it in Morphe.
3. Uninstall the official AARE (the patched app has a different signature), install the patched one and set AARE up.
4. Turn the patches on in **AARE → Settings → Settings** (the last row).
5. For Voice messages: keep [Shizuku](https://github.com/RikkaApps/Shizuku) running and allowed for AARE.

Patching again later (a new release of the patches) installs over the patched AARE and keeps its settings.

## ✅ In AARE itself

Some patches were proposed to AARE and are now part of it, so they are no longer here:

- **Background location** (navigation with the phone locked): AARE 1.6.0, [#351](https://github.com/prototux/AARE/pull/351).
- **Car microphone** for dictation: AARE 1.6.0's *Car microphone for phone apps*, [#256](https://github.com/prototux/AARE/pull/256).
- **Reply by voice**: proposed for AARE 1.6.1, [#353](https://github.com/prototux/AARE/pull/353).

Tunnel navigation and Voice messages are tested here first, then proposed to AARE.

The patches for **AARE 1.5.0** (with the AARE assistant and Integrated Shizuku) are in [v1.10.6](https://github.com/Pirrotto/aare-patches/releases/tag/v1.10.6), the last release for 1.5.0.

**English**

- **2.0 – For AARE 1.6.0.** Background location and Car microphone are now in AARE itself. The AARE assistant and Integrated Shizuku are left out. Tunnel navigation: honest accuracy, 5 km / 5 minute limit, queues, tests. Voice messages: the helper picks the one recording, AARE sees no other file. In a tunnel the position no longer freezes when the car repeats its last fix. Outside tunnels it learns how much the car's gyroscope under- or over-states curves (and its speed against the GPS), so in tunnels curves are followed instead of drifting to their outside; a short gap in the car's speed no longer stops the estimate. In tunnels longer than about 750 m the arrow no longer stops: the accuracy given to the apps stays under the 50 m beyond which Vela ignores a position. Settings → "Copy diagnostics" copies a technical log (no position, names or texts; kept in AARE's private storage after AARE closes, newest 1000 lines) to send when something goes wrong. "Delete diagnostics" deletes it. The voice features (voice messages, voice reply) need Android 8 or later; on Android 6-7 they stay off and say so in Settings. Reply after a WhatsApp voice message works again. "Reply" in the notification centre also works after WhatsApp took its notification away (for 1 hour, while the car stays connected; wiped when it disconnects). Closing the reply card on the car screen ends the reply and stops the read-back; a long dictated reply is no longer cut off (15 s to start talking, 60 s at most). New setting "Ask by voice to reply": AARE asks "Do you want to reply?" after reading and a spoken yes starts the dictation (off by default). Dictation goes on through short pauses and ends after 2.5 s of silence; "eee", "ehm", "mmm" are left out. A clear yes or no to "Send it?" acts at once; if it isn't understood, it is asked once more. The music stays paused for the whole reply, and the spoken phrases are shorter. New setting "Listening beep" (off by default): a short tone going up when it starts listening and going down when it stops, as Android Auto's assistant does. With "Ask by voice to reply" on, the reply speaks as Android Auto's Google voice does: "Marco says: …", "Do you want to reply?", "What's the message?", "Here's your message to Marco: … Do you want to send it?", "Message sent to Marco."; "change message" dictates it again; so do "retry", "redo", "wrong" ("riprova", "rifai", "sbagliato", "nochmal"). Voice reply is now off by default, as AARE wants for new features: if it was on and you never touched its switch, switch it on again once in the patches' Settings. Tunnel navigation remembers what it learned for each car model, so the first tunnel of a trip already follows curves; switching it off deletes it. After the car disconnects or the link drops, the estimate no longer starts from where the car was before. New patch "Car radio pause" (off by default): while AARE reads a message or a voice reply runs, the car's radio pauses until AARE is done instead of only getting a little lower. Test switch "Location check" under Tunnel navigation (off by default): notes whether the navigation apps may read the location, for the black map at the start. Test switch "Location for navigation at the start" (off by default, needs Shizuku): has the Shizuku shell start a small location service in AARE when the car connects, so the navigation app gets the location without opening AARE on the phone. In tunnels the estimate also corrects the car gyroscope's small zero offset, learned on straights, which turned it off the road towards the end of long tunnels; the diagnostics now say how many degrees the heading was off when the GPS comes back. After the next test (25.11) the offset made it worse (59 degrees off): it is no longer used, only shown beside two other ways in the diagnostics. The estimate now reports its honest accuracy (10 % of the distance, as measured) and stops before it passes 45 m, about 400 m into a tunnel, instead of sending positions far off the road. After it stops, the estimate goes on unsent, only for the diagnostics: when the GPS comes back they say how far off it was over the whole tunnel, in metres per 100 m, to tell whether it could honestly be sent further. Nothing more is sent to the car or the apps. Voice reply sounds more natural: on Android 13 and later the dictation gets punctuation and capitals from the phone's speech recognition, a piece dictated after a full stop starts with a capital, and the read-back is shorter: "To Marco: … Send it?".

## 🧑‍💻 Development

- Day-to-day work goes to `dev`: every push runs the unit tests, builds the patches and applies them to the official AARE APK, and keeps the patches file as a private test build (an artifact of the run, not a release).
- Public releases come from `main`, at most twice a week (Thursday evening, Saturday evening or Sunday morning), after testing in the car. Commits follow [semantic commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org) (`feat:`, `fix:`, `chore:`).
- Local build: `./gradlew buildAndroid`, then `patches/build/libs/patches-*.mpp`.

Based on the [Morphe patches template](https://github.com/MorpheApp/morphe-patches-template). Not affiliated with Morphe or with AARE.
